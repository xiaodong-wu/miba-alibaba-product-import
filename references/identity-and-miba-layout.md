## Latest Alibaba image-reference requirement
The user wants product-accurate, independently designed images, not close remakes. Original Alibaba gallery/detail images must be imagegen inputs for product identity and function, NOT templates for the overall composition. Preserve silhouette, proportions, joints, ports, materials and actual device contact. Redesign framing, subject placement/scale, setting/props, lighting, palette and text hierarchy. Background recoloring or copy replacement alone is insufficient. Do not reproduce distinctive source effects, giant numerals, arrow layouts or thumbnail grids.
Record identity_locks and design_changes separately for every image. Review product fidelity and visual independence separately; failure of either requires revision. Retaining an observed product angle is appropriate; inventing unseen geometry to look different is not. The user's “80% similarity” was criticism of an overly similar result, not a target, measured metric or permission to promise a percentage. Never use rejected generated images as the identity source.

## Product identity before generation
- Record reference_identity from this product's real photographs, video frames and labels: variant, silhouette, proportions, joints, ports, markings, material and finish. Keep each selected source image variant internally consistent; use physical photos to flag discrepancies, not to silently replace the requested visual.
- Never use a previously rejected AI image as the identity reference or final asset.
- Every imagegen call must use the inspected original Alibaba image assigned to that output role. Lock their observed geometry and viewpoint. Keep the actual body, hinge, arm thickness, charging rings and port positions.
- Both product fidelity and independent visual design are required. Preserve the product itself; redesign the composition around it substantially. Do not confuse product redesign with image-design originality.
- Do not combine inconsistent structures from different variants. Do not invent unseen rear or bottom surfaces. Use observed states and views to build one cover and at least four distinct gallery images.
- Application images must retain correct device contact, support and cable connections. Do not move a phone away from the actual charging pad center, add charging surfaces, imply unverified simultaneous charging or create floating parts.
- Compare every output with its real reference at a similar orientation. Check silhouette, proportions, connections and markings separately from visual appeal. Record accepted/rejected status and reasons. A generated=true flag is not evidence of fidelity.
- For a failed image, attempt at most two targeted corrections. If still inaccurate, report that asset blocked and retain drafts separately. Do not upload an inaccurate image as a successful result or silently substitute a source image.
- Empty-product shots may explain structure, but an entire set of empty products in decorative rooms is not sufficient application coverage.
